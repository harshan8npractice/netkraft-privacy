Privacy Policy — NetKraft

Effective date: December 27, 2025

NetKraft (“NetKraft”, “we”, “our”, “us”) provides WhatsApp-based business automation services for merchants (e.g., order intake, notifications, and customer support automation).

Information we collect

  • Message content sent to a connected WhatsApp Business number (e.g., customer messages, attachments such as images).
  • Contact details such as phone numbers and profile identifiers needed to deliver messages.
  • Business information provided by the merchant (e.g., store name, operating hours, menu/catalog, configuration settings).
  • Order and interaction data related to business workflows (e.g., order items, pickup/delivery preference, timestamps).
  • Technical data such as logs and diagnostics to operate and secure the service.

How we use information

  • Provide and operate NetKraft’s automation services.
  • Send and receive messages through WhatsApp on behalf of the connected business.
  • Support merchants (troubleshooting, configuration, and reporting).
  • Improve reliability, prevent abuse, and secure our systems.

How information is shared

We do not sell personal information.

We share information only as needed to provide the service, including with:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (WhatsApp) to send/receive messages via the WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • Service providers that help us operate the platform (e.g., hosting, logging, databases).

Data retention

We retain information only as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain security/audit logs, and comply with legal obligations.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information. No system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

International transfers

If you use our services, information may be processed in countries other than your own, where data protection laws may differ.
